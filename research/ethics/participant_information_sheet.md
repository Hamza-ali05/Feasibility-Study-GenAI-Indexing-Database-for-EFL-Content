# Participant Information Sheet (template)

**Study title:** Feasibility of a GenAI indexing database for EFL content  
**Institution:** Canterbury Christ Church University  
**Researcher:** Muhammad Yousaf  

## What is this study?
You are invited to try a prototype search system for English-language teaching materials and to discuss whether the results are pedagogically useful.

## What will I do?
- Use the prototype for about 15–20 minutes (search, filters, Ask AI).
- Complete a short System Usability Scale (SUS) form.
- Join a 30–45 minute interview (online or in person).
- Optionally comment on whether results seem biased toward certain CEFR levels or skills.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason, up to two weeks after interview, by emailing the researcher. Your transcript and scores will then be deleted.

## What data is kept?
- Anonymised transcript (pseudonym P1, P2, … — never your real name)
- SUS scores and demographic band (teaching context, years of experience)
- No audio is stored in the project repository

Data is stored on CCCU-approved systems for 12 months after the project ends, then deleted.

## Consent
You will sign a consent form before the interview. A copy of this sheet is yours to keep.
